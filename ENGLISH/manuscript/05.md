## 5

Twelve battery packs for commercial radios were sitting on Daniel's list of open items.

Delivery address: a trading company in Warehouse C, Unit 17.

Jonas looked at the item. "Twelve?"

"Pack of twelve."

"Price?"

"Four hundred eighty before tax."

"That's disappointingly normal."

Daniel opened the commercial register. Technical wholesale and retail, event and communications equipment. Same managing director since the company was founded. Two published annual accounts.

Nothing about it was interesting.

Then Daniel looked at the address again.

His eyes stopped on the address, not the name.

"We've seen this before."

Jonas stepped closer.

They found the old case within seconds: the same warehouse complex, seven months earlier. A small van with forged registration papers had been parked there for two nights. The case had been closed as fraud. No terrorism link.

Daniel opened the site plan.

"Where was the van?"

"Building A," Jonas said.

Daniel pointed to the current delivery address. "And this is C."

Three buildings. Twenty-three units. Not even the same entrance.

"So about as much the same address as a parking garage," Jonas said.

"About."

Daniel had the company checked through open sources. Website, phone number, business purpose, delivery address. No special database query, no application, no surveillance.

Forty minutes later, Jonas came back.

"Unfortunately, still boring."

The website had been around for years. Phone number and legal notice matched the register. The landlord confirmed that the company had used Unit 17 for more than two years and regularly received goods there. The supplier listed the batteries as a standard shipment. No cash payment. No special request.

Daniel looked at the old vehicle case again.

"Different company," Jonas said.

"Yes."

"Different building."

"Yes."

"Different matter."

"Yes."

"Then close it?"

Daniel closed the company name and the order. He left a free-text note on the address: **Warehouse complex appeared in a separate, closed matter; different section of the site, no common security link. If new information emerges, include the address connection in the review.**

The system offered more. Observation flag. Scheduled review. Linkage-relevant.

He selected none of them.

Jonas read the note. "What if nothing new ever comes in?"

"Then it stays a sentence nobody needs."

Daniel closed the screen and pulled the next item into the center.

Ten minutes later, he had almost forgotten the warehouse address.

Only the marker remained.

Warehouse C, Unit 17.
